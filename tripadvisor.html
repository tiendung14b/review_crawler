Somewhat mixed review

Aug 2026 • Couples
Very large night market that gave us a mixed impression. There is a good atmosphere there and it is pleasant to look at the different stalls.
On the other hand, the choice for dining is very limited compared to the night markets we visited during our vacation in Malaysia in 2025. Here, you should expect a lot of clothes, jewelry, and souvenirs for tourists. The stalls sell roughly the same items (inexpensive, it is true) and in the end, there is nothing very authentic.
Furthermore, the space to move around is very limited and unfortunately, scooters continue to drive in the street. I dare not imagine the chaos in high season.
